# Flowdeck Knowledge Base

## Company

Flowdeck is operations software for logistics and 3PL companies. It was founded in 2023 in Bengaluru by a team that ran a small 3PL themselves before building the product. They spent two years stitching together spreadsheets, a warehouse tool, and a separate invoicing process, and kept losing hours every week just making sure the numbers agreed across all three. Flowdeck exists to remove that gap.

The product covers four areas: warehouse management, fleet and dispatch, order orchestration, and billing reconciliation. Customers range from single warehouse operators to logistics companies running ten or more locations.

## Product

Flowdeck has four core modules.

**Warehouse management** gives real time stock levels across every location, with alerts when inventory runs low and a unified view for operators managing more than one warehouse.

**Fleet and dispatch** tracks vehicles, assigns routes, and gives live delivery status. Dispatchers see which driver is closest to a new order and assign it without leaving the dashboard.

**Order orchestration** takes an order in from any sales channel and routes it to the warehouse best placed to fulfill it, based on stock and location. Status updates sync back automatically, so the customer always sees accurate delivery information.

**Billing reconciliation** is the module Flowdeck customers mention most. Every delivered order is automatically matched against the corresponding carrier invoice and warehouse handling charge. If a carrier bills for a weight or a distance that does not match the actual shipment, or if a handling charge appears for an order that was never fulfilled, Flowdeck flags it before the invoice is paid. Most logistics teams do this matching by hand in a spreadsheet, checking a handful of invoices a week if they have time. Flowdeck checks every single one, automatically, the same day the invoice arrives. Teams using this module have caught billing errors worth several percent of their total shipping spend within the first month, money that would otherwise have been paid without anyone noticing.

Reconciliation works the same way regardless of company size. A single warehouse operator gets the same automatic matching as a ten warehouse operation, the difference between tiers is volume and support, not which checks run.

## Pricing

Flowdeck has 4 tiers, based on warehouse count and monthly order volume.

**Starter**
- 1 warehouse
- Up to 500 orders per month
- All 4 core modules included
- Email support
- Most Starter customers are a single site that just moved off spreadsheets. The reconciliation module alone tends to justify the switch at this size, since it is the one thing a small team has no time to do by hand.

**Growth**
- 2 to 5 warehouses
- Up to 5,000 orders per month
- All 4 core modules included
- Priority support
- This is where multi warehouse stock visibility starts to matter. Teams at this tier are usually the ones who feel the most pain from tools that do not talk to each other, since they are coordinating across locations without a system built for it.

**Scale**
- 6 to 10 warehouses
- Up to 20,000 orders per month
- All 4 core modules included
- Faster support response times
- Dedicated onboarding specialist for data migration
- At this size, reconciliation catches the most in absolute terms, simply because there are more invoices to check every day. Teams here often ask about the onboarding specialist first, since migrating 6 or more warehouses of existing data is the main thing standing between them and going live.

**Gold**
- 10+ warehouses
- 20,000+ orders per month
- All 4 core modules included
- Dedicated account manager
- Custom support tailored to volume
- Gold accounts typically have the most complex carrier mix, multiple carriers across regions, which is exactly where manual reconciliation breaks down first. This is also the only tier with a named account manager rather than a support queue.

Flowdeck does not publish exact pricing, since the right number depends on warehouse count, order volume, and which modules matter most to a given team. The way to get an exact quote is to fill out the contact form on the website. Someone from the team follows up directly with real numbers based on the specifics.

Tier is set by whichever number is higher, warehouse count or order volume, not whichever is lower. A team with 3 warehouses and only 400 orders a month is still Growth, not Starter, because 3 warehouses already exceeds the Starter limit of 1. The same works the other way, a single warehouse doing 4,000 orders a month is Growth as well, even though it is only 1 location, because order volume has crossed the Starter threshold.

## Differentiation

Most tools in this space solve one problem. There are warehouse management systems that only manage warehouses, dispatch tools that only handle fleet routing, and separate invoicing software that only does billing. A growing logistics company usually ends up running three or four of these side by side, and then someone on the team spends hours every week making sure the numbers from each one actually agree.

Flowdeck is built as one system instead of four. Warehouse, fleet, orders, and billing reconciliation all read from the same data, so nothing has to be manually cross checked between tools. When an order moves from warehouse to delivery, the same record carries through to reconciliation, and if a carrier invoice does not match what actually happened, that gets caught automatically instead of during a manual audit weeks later.

The reconciliation module in particular is rare. Most competitors, including the larger warehouse management platforms, treat billing as something you export and hand off to accounting. Flowdeck treats it as part of the operational loop, checked the same day, on every order, not a sample.

## Frequently asked

**How long does onboarding take?**
Most teams are live within two to three weeks, including migrating existing data from spreadsheets or another platform. Scale and Gold customers get a dedicated onboarding specialist for this.

**Is my data secure?**
Flowdeck is SOC 2 Type II compliant, with data encrypted at rest and in transit. Scale and Gold plans include a 99.9 percent uptime SLA.

**Can I see exact pricing without talking to sales?**
Not published pricing, no. Warehouse count, order volume, and which modules matter most all affect the plan, so the accurate way to get a number is the contact form. It takes a minute and someone follows up with real figures for your setup.

**Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?**
Starter and Growth are billed monthly or annually with no long term lock in. Scale and Gold typically run on an annual commitment, reflecting the dedicated support that comes with those tiers.
